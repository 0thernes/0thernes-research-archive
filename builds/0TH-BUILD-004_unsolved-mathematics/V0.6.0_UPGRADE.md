## Claim boundary

The v0.6.0 release records bounded structural theorems, witnesses, and
refutations across eight open-problem domains. It explicitly does **not** claim
to solve the grand open conjectures catalogued by the Atlas. This snapshot is
therefore evidence for related formal progress, not a retirement of the
corresponding Mega-Atlas grand problems.

The newly visible bounded results include:

- the complete Lonely Runner statement through `n ≤ 3` for the stated positive
  triple domain;
- Euclid's general even-perfect-number theorem under its explicit hypotheses;
- exact finite Ramsey results including `R(3,3)=6` and `R(3,4)=9`;
- structural, modular, witness, and adversarial-refutation theorems for the
  Collatz, Erdős–Straus, Brocard, prime/twin-prime, Goldbach, odd-perfect, and
  Mersenne-problem dossiers.

These are finite, structural, or conditional results. They do **not** prove the
full Collatz, Erdős–Straus, Lonely Runner, Brocard, twin-prime, Goldbach,
odd-perfect-number, or Mersenne-prime infinitude conjectures.
