### R2. Digit-sum characterisation of two-register passive register programs with F_p constants

- **Statement.** For p prime, t ≥ 1, f ∈ F_p[x]: a passive two-register program (instructions O += P(R), R += c, O += λx, R += λx; constants in F_p) with at most t input accesses producing O + f(x) and restoring R, as an identity of formal polynomials in F_p[R, x], exists exactly when every d ≥ 2 with [x^d]f ≠ 0 has base-p digit sum s_p(d) ≤ min(p−1, t−1).
- **Evidence.** Lean kernel; declarations [`Catalytic.Solution.t1_digit_sum_dichotomy`](https://github.com/0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers/blob/4bfeabb05a960450114f975f477259ccf189cdf1/lean/Solution.lean#L17), [`Catalytic.Challenge.T1_DigitSumDichotomy`](https://github.com/0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers/blob/4bfeabb05a960450114f975f477259ccf189cdf1/lean/Challenge.lean#L21), [`Catalytic.rp_pass_two_iff`](https://github.com/0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers/blob/4bfeabb05a960450114f975f477259ccf189cdf1/lean/Catalytic/DigitSumDichotomy.lean#L952), [`Catalytic.CleanlyComputes2`](https://github.com/0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers/blob/4bfeabb05a960450114f975f477259ccf189cdf1/lean/Catalytic/DigitSumDichotomy.lean#L117), [`Catalytic.Solution.t1s_pass2_ring_soundness`](https://github.com/0thernes/catalytic-register-frontiers/blob/4bfeabb05a960450114f975f477259ccf189cdf1/lean/Solution.lean#L37); axioms: propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. - **Audit (2026-11-26).** Rebuilt from a clean clone at the audited commit: pass; the formal statement matches the stated claim; prior work: no earlier proof found.
- **Limit.** Cleanliness means equality of formal polynomials; as functions on F_p itself x^p = x, so for example x^3 over F_2 is computed with one input access although the theorem excludes it.

## Not claimed

- No Millennium Prize problem and no grand open problem is solved here. P versus NP is open.
- No result has been refereed by a journal or checked by a named outside expert.
- Engineering invariant suites (section F) verify software models. They are not evidence about consciousness, sentience, biology or physics.
- A search that found no earlier proof is not a proof of novelty.

## How a result gets on this list

1. The claim is registered in the archive with a pinned commit and a frozen paper.
2. The formal statement is read against the informal claim and against the cited source, in a session that did not write the proof.
3. The proof is rebuilt from a clean clone at that commit, or the ledger says it was not.
4. The literature is searched for earlier or parallel proofs and the outcome is recorded.
5. The wording is limited to what steps 2 to 4 support. Anything narrower than the usual informal claim is stated under Limit.

## How to challenge a result

- Open an issue on this repository or on the result's repository and quote the result number (for example R7).
- A reproducible counterexample, a gap in a statement audit, or an earlier proof in the literature leads to a correction: the result is narrowed, reclassified or withdrawn, and keeps its number.
- Corrections are recorded in CHANGELOG.md.
